# Star character (archived sketches)

The yellow star character with big eyes was the first idea for Spark's mascot. The brand has since moved to a more mature, 18+ look: the logo is now the royal-blue wordmark with a gradient sparkle, and the character is **not part of the brand system**. The two sheets stay in `assets/Mascot/` as reference only.

## Status

- Do not use the character in the logo, app icon, avatars, buttons or any core screen.
- If the concept video needs a character moment, treat it as optional sticker-style art, one pose at a time, kept small, on white or a `mint` wash. Check with the team first.
- The sheets use coral hearts and warm orange feet that are not in the six brand colors; do not borrow those colors elsewhere.
- Do not redraw or restyle the character. If a mature mascot is wanted later, start from a new brief rather than editing these sheets.
