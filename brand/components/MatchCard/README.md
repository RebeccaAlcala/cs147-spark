The reveal shown to both people when two check-ins at the same place are matched. It shows interests and an age range only.

**Parts**
- Anonymous avatar: the sky-to-mint sparkle on an `ink` circle (`sp-avatar`). Never a photo at this stage.
- yellow `sp-badge--match` badge, a title naming the place, the age range, interest chips.
- A privacy note in `caption` text and two actions: "Not now" (secondary) and "Say hi" (yellow accept).

**You provide**
- The place name, an age range (such as "25 to 30"), and 3 to 5 shared or complementary interests.
- Handlers for accept and decline. Both people must accept before chat opens.

**Do**
- Set it on the `sp-wash` mint gradient and use the hero treatment (`radius-xl`, `shadow-lift`).
- Always keep the privacy note visible; it is what makes the feature feel safe.

**Don't**
- Don't show names, photos, exact ages, social handles or any score.
- Don't use "Match" language that implies attraction; this is for friendships and spontaneous meetups.
