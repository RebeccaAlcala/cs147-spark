How it works

Three steps from "I'm here" to hello

1

Pick your interests

Answer a short questionnaire. We only share interests and an age range.

2

Check in nearby

Spark points you to third spaces close by. Tap "I'm here" when you arrive.

3

Meet someone friendly

If someone at the same spot shares your interests and you both say hi, chat just long enough to find each other.
