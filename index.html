Say hi to someone new

About

A friendly nudge, never pressure to perform

Plenty of us spend time in the same cafes, climbing gyms and game stores as people we would probably get along with, but nobody knows how to say hello.

Spark points you to nearby third spaces and lets you check in when you arrive. If someone checked in at the same spot shares your interests, you are both introduced. You each see only interests and an age range. There is no messaging: if you both say hi, you meet right where you are, right away.

How it works

Three steps from "I'm here" to hello

1

Pick your interests

Answer a short questionnaire. We only share interests and an age range.

2

Check in nearby

Spark points you to third spaces close by. Tap "I'm here" when you arrive.

3

Meet someone friendly

If someone at the same spot shares your interests and you both say hi, you meet right there. No messaging, just hello.
